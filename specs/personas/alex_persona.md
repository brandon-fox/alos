# BDD Persona: Alex - Busy Executive / Remote Knowledge Worker

## Profile
- **Name:** Alex
- **Role:** Tech Executive & Remote Knowledge Worker
- **Goal:** Minimize administrative life friction, maintain an organized calendar, automate Todoist task workflows, and delegate research to an autonomous assistant without risking unintended mutations or privacy leaks.

## Key Behaviors & Expectations
1. **Privacy-First:** Demands that personal notes, house details, and preferences stay on local disk in Markdown format.
2. **Deterministic & Safe:** Requires explicit confirmation for sending emails or deleting calendar events, but expects low-risk tasks (task drafts, schedule queries, note summaries) to execute completely autonomously.
3. **Self-Correcting:** Expects ALOS to remember past corrections (e.g. "No meetings after 5 PM", "Prefer Delta over United") and never repeat a mistake recorded in `CORRECTION_LEDGER.md`.
4. **Audit Trail:** Expects complete visibility into why actions were taken via an immutable system log.
